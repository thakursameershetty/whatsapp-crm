How it works

The whole campaign, in four steps.

From the spreadsheet on your desktop to the replies on your phone, without the busywork in between.

  1. Step 1 · Upload

    Drop in your sheet

  2. Step 2 · Personalise

    Answer every reply

    • One shared inbox

FAQ

Questions, answered.

The ones every team asks before they send their first campaign. Pull a card to read it.

SafetyQ.01Will my number get blocked?

Messages go through Meta's official WhatsApp Business API, using templates Meta has approved, and are sent at a safe pace. Each number's quality rating from Meta is shown in the app, so you can see how it's doing.

Filed under Safety · Q.01

SetupQ.02Do I need a developer?

No. You need a WhatsApp Business number on Meta's Cloud API: its phone number ID and an access token from Meta Business Settings. Paste them under Numbers and the app checks them with Meta before saving.

Filed under Setup · Q.02

MessagingQ.03Can I message people who haven't messaged me first?

Yes, with a template Meta has approved. Once someone replies, you can write to them freely for 24 hours from the inbox.

Filed under Messaging · Q.03

TemplatesQ.04Do I have to create templates in Meta?

No. You can create and edit them in the app, with a live preview. They're sent to Meta for review and their status updates here by itself. Templates you already made in Meta sync in with one click.

Filed under Templates · Q.04

SheetsQ.05My lead sheets are messy. Is that a problem?

No. The app reads several numbers in one cell, ignores notes like “(unclear)”, treats “Not Captured” and “N/A” as empty unless you say otherwise, and shows you exactly which rows need attention.

Filed under Sheets · Q.05

TeamQ.06Can my team use it together?

Yes. Invite teammates to a workspace as owners or members. Each business gets its own workspace, and an owner can protect one with a password.

Filed under Team · Q.06

AIQ.07Does the AI send messages on its own?

Only if you turn on auto-reply for a conversation. Otherwise it suggests a reply for you to send or edit. It never starts a campaign without your approval.

Filed under AI · Q.07
