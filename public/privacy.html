Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data SendON handles, why, who it's shared with and the choices you have. We collect only what we need to run the service, we don't sell personal data, and we don't use advertising trackers.

1. Who we are

SendON is provided by Spotmies LLP, India ("Spotmies", "we", "us"). This policy covers the SendON app and website. It is written with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 in mind.

2. Whose data it is

  • Your account data (your name, email and how you use SendON): we decide how it's used, so we are responsible for it.
  • Your customers' data (the contacts you upload or who message your numbers, and your conversations with them): your business decides how it's used, and we process it on your behalf, only to provide SendON to you. Your business is responsible for having their consent and for answering their requests. If you're one of those customers, please contact the business that messaged you; we'll help them respond.

4. How we use it

  • To provide SendON: send your campaigns and replies through Meta, show your inbox and reports, and run the AI features you use.
  • To keep accounts secure: sign-in codes, rate limits, and detecting misuse.
  • To send service emails, such as confirmation codes, invites, password resets and important notices.
  • To manage credits and keep billing records.
  • To support you, and to understand and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use your customers' data for our own marketing.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, to protect people's safety or rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

6. AI features

When you use the Assistant or the Inbox lead assistant, the text, conversation history and files needed for that request are sent to the AI provider you use. We send only what the request needs. If you connect your own AI provider key, that provider's own terms and privacy policy also apply.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. Your browser's local storage remembers display preferences such as theme and layout. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

8. How long we keep it

  • We keep your data while your account and workspaces exist.
  • When a workspace is deleted, its data (numbers, contacts, campaigns, messages and files) is deleted. When your account is deleted, your account data is deleted, along with workspaces only you used.
  • Copies in backups are overwritten in the normal course of time. We keep billing records for as long as the law requires.

9. Security

We use HTTPS, store passwords only as salted hashes, encrypt stored WhatsApp access tokens and AI keys, limit access by role inside each workspace, and rate-limit sign-in and sending. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, ask us to stop using it, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance with us. You can correct most details yourself in Settings and request account deletion from Settings → Account. For anything else, email connect@sendon.si. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Where it's processed

Some of our providers store or process data outside India. We use providers that protect data to a standard consistent with this policy and with Indian law.

12. Children

SendON is for businesses and isn't meant for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect children's data from account holders.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is significant, we'll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
