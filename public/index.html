Getting a Gemini key

Gemini by Google has a free tier, enough for development and demos.

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it here, click Save, then Test.

On the free tier Google may use what you send to improve its products, and requests per minute and per day are limited. Avoid real customer data, or switch to Claude.

Getting a Claude key

Claude by Anthropic is paid per use (a few cents per request) and isn't included in a Claude.ai subscription.

  1. Sign in or sign up at console.anthropic.com.
  2. Add credit under Settings → Billing; you can set a monthly limit there.
  3. Open Settings → API keys → Create key and copy it. It's only shown once.
  4. Paste it here, click Save, then Test.

Connecting to Meta

Five steps to get your API credentials, once per business.

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps, create an app of type Business and add the WhatsApp product.
  2. In WhatsApp → API Setup, add and verify each business phone number. Copy each number's Phone number ID. The WhatsApp Business Account ID is found automatically when you add the number.
  3. Create a permanent token: Business Settings → Users → System users → add an Admin system user → assign your app and WhatsApp account → Generate token with whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management.
  4. Create message templates in WhatsApp Manager → Message templates and wait for approval.
  5. Here: Numbers → Add number for each phone, Templates → Sync from Meta, then switch Demo mode off.

Meta limits how many unique people your business can message outside the 24-hour reply window in a rolling 24 hours. The limit is shared by every number in your business portfolio, starts at 250 and rises (2,000, 10,000, 100,000, unlimited) while your quality stays high. Only message people who opted in. Blocks and reports lower your quality rating and can get numbers restricted.
